# about

A quick word about myself. Pun intended.
---
I was the founding engineer at Sensei, a Sutter-Hill Venture backed startup, for a couple of years before it shut down in March 2026. We were building an AI-enabled sales-tech platform for the modern sales reps. I did all kinds of work at Sensei, ranging from building a real-time engine for processing audio and transcripts really fast, working on a knowledge graph for AI systems, writing client-side React, and the best of all, writing a C++ integration of our system with Zoom to pull AV in real-time from meetings. Sensei was a fun ride.

Before Sensei, I was a SWE at smallcase, a Series-C fintech startup in India. I was part of the team that built Loan Against Mutual Funds (LAMF) and helped the team scale it from 0-1. I wrote a bunch of JavaScript and a lot of Golang while I was there. Smallcase gave me a taste of what a real company looks like.

I started my journey in technology with Swadesh, which was a YC-backed startup building a neo-bank for Indian expats in The US. I joined Swadesh as a founding engineer and got to work on amazing problems and understand what starting a company looks like from the inside, compared to hearing about it from the sidelines.

Ultimately, I am always curious about what's going to happen next and don't hold much value to my current skillset. Vibe-coding is the new kid around the block these days so why not give it a shot? At the end of the day, one of my core beliefs is to build and create with whatever tools you have at your disposal.
